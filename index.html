Architecture decisions and plans. Every record is Proposed until the developer has reviewed it and the owner has signed it off; see the architecture map and glossary for what each status means.

  • Engineering rulebookThe 43 rules every change must keep, each a one-line summary of a decision a record makes, with a link to it. The record wins on any difference. Load it with every coding task.
  • Architecture map and glossaryHow the records fit together, one word per concept, and what each status (Proposed, Accepted, Implemented, Superseded, Deprecated) means. Every record is Proposed today.
  • ADR-000: System architectureA modular monolith of modules with typed ports, one core that owns stock and orders, an outbox, one egress gate, connector plugins and simulators. Proposed, 17 October 2026.
  • ADR-001: Multi-tenant database architectureControl database, shared-table customer databases with forced row-level security, a wrong-database check on every transaction, dedicated databases, trials as a state. Proposed 17 October 2026, revised 18 October 2026.
  • ADR-002: Data retention and archiving24 months of history in each cell, older months archived per company in object storage, statutory retention, then deletion. Proposed, 17 October 2026.
  • ADR-003: Product modelSKU identity, barcodes, simple products, packs with their own stock, bundles built from components, box sizes, size and colour families, VAT classes, best-before and lot tracking, listing links including FBA and Vendor, and how products are created. Proposed, 17 October 2026.
  • ADR-004: Channel connectorsEach marketplace is a translator behind a small contract (read orders, read listings, write stock, read back); the host owns credentials, HTTP, rate limits and shared daily allowances; orders arrive by notice; simulators, shadow mode, and failures kept per account. Proposed, 17 October 2026.
    • ADR-004.1: eBay connectorOne app every seller approves, orders by eBay's Notification API, the Inventory API and the Trading API for every linked listing, the shared Trading pool, the out-of-stock option, account deletion notices. Proposed, 17 October 2026.
    • ADR-004.2: Amazon Seller connectorSeller Central approval renewed yearly, one account per seller and region, the AWS notice relay, every stock write a single listing update at launch, read back on every write, no figure for listings Amazon ships, buyer data. Proposed, 17 October 2026.
    • ADR-004.3: Shopify connectorInstalling Vervoro's app, expiring tokens, one warehouse location, webhooks to one address, compare-and-set writes in batches of 250, a cost-point budget, variants without a unique SKU, orders other marketplaces put into the shop, privacy webhooks. Proposed, 18 October 2026.
  • ADR-005: Catalogue sync and linkingListings keyed by the marketplace's own id, a full read on connect plus notices, a daily safety read and refresh, two proofs before a listing ends, suggestions in three tiers with vetoes, links made only by a person and kept as versions, unlinked listings left to the marketplace, products from listings, Amazon offers by ASIN. Proposed, 18 October 2026.
  • ADR-006: Stock ledger and reservationsAn append-only ledger that only its own entries can change, proved every night; four numbers per product, batch and spot (on hand, reserved, held, sellable); reserving a batch on import, earliest sell-by date first among batches with the shelf life the demand needs; packing that records the batch that left; use-by dates; bundles in whole sets; counts while picking goes on; returns, receiving, no negative stock, one lock per company and commands safe to repeat. Proposed 18 October 2026, revised after review the same day.
  • ADR-007: Order ingestionNotices first with the backup read behind, one order per marketplace order, one status list mapped from every marketplace, holds beside the status, reserving on import oldest purchase first, cancellation requests that keep stock, cancellations made only on the marketplace (Vervoro never sends one), holds in three kinds, unlinked and marketplace-shipped orders, orders shipped outside Vervoro, manual orders, and what dispatch is handed. Proposed, 18 October 2026.
  • ADR-008: Inventory syncThe figure per listing from one warehouse's sellable stock, near zero every change sent at once and above it only a gap of 20 (the reverse cap), raises only after a complete order read, one belief per listing that errs only upward, every write confirmed, eBay's 250 revisions a day, single Amazon patches, the last 2 units kept for one account, one stop question, one switch day off an old system, and recovery. Proposed, 18 October 2026.
  • ADR-009: Buyer data and privacyThe seller as controller and Vervoro as processor, only what a label needs, one place and three layers of encryption, views by role and order state and every view logged, anonymised 30 days after delivery, VAT invoices kept, buyers' requests through the seller or marketplace, erasure through backups and restores, incidents within 24 hours, the security schedule marketplaces ask for, and each marketplace's rules. Proposed, 18 October 2026.
  • ADR-010: Users, roles and sign-inOne login per person across companies, a password with an authenticator app code or a passkey, one person per phone, sessions that follow the shift, recovery through the company first, six fixed roles with warehouse scope in one permission matrix, read-only operator grants the seller sees and can end, two audit trails, and no API tokens at launch. Sign-in is built by Vervoro, with no identity provider, in a Worker of its own that alone holds the sign-in keys. Proposed, 18 October 2026; revised the same day.
  • ADR-011: Invoicing and VATThe seller as supplier and one issuer per sale, Amazon invoicing its own orders, documents only when needed or asked for, a VAT record for every sale line from import, Vervoro's own VAT beside the marketplace's with differences flagged, per-line rounding, hampers split by cost and delivery by value, one gap-free series, frozen PDFs, credit notes only against Vervoro's invoices, a CSV export split by issuer, and UK-only scope with exports zero-rated. Proposed, 18 October 2026.
  • ADR-012: Billing and plansPlans as rows of modules with a soft order ceiling averaged over three months and no overage, unlimited users and connections, a 21-day trial with no card until a connection goes live, four states computed from dates with the stock path running until closing, closing by the seller's stop choice and then figures that only fall until erasure, reopening without losing anything, Stripe with cards and Bacs Direct Debit on hosted pages (SAQ A), upgrades at once and downgrades at renewal, one invoice sequence, UK VAT, and erasure 30 days after closing. Proposed, 18 October 2026.

Build order

The order to build in. Each step needs the ones before it.

  1. ADR-000: System architecture, ADR-001: Multi-tenant database architecture
    The skeleton: modules and ports, the outbox, the egress gate, tenancy and row-level security. Every later record is a module on top.
  2. ADR-010: Users, roles and sign-in
    Users, roles and sign-in are part of the skeleton: every later module names its actions, and this record decides who holds them. Sign-in, sessions and operator grants exist before any screen shows a buyer's address.
  3. ADR-003: Product model
    Products come first; listings, stock and orders all point at them.
  4. ADR-006: Stock ledger and reservations
    Stock is recorded and reserved inside Vervoro before any marketplace sees a figure. Only the foundation writes it.
  5. ADR-004: Channel connectors, ADR-004.1: eBay connector, ADR-004.2: Amazon Seller connector, ADR-004.3: Shopify connector
    The connector host first, then eBay and Amazon Seller side by side, then Shopify. Each runs against its simulator in shadow mode. Amazon Vendor (ADR-004.4, planned) comes later.
  6. ADR-005: Catalogue sync and linking
    Needs the connectors' listing reads and the products to link them to.
  7. ADR-009: Buyer data and privacy
    Before any real order stores a buyer's address; the marketplaces' data reviews ask for it. Buyer anonymisation from ADR-002 is built here.
  8. ADR-007: Order ingestion
    Needs links, reservations and the buyer-data rules.
  9. ADR-011: Invoicing and VAT
    Invoices follow orders: needs order lines, refunds, the buyer-data rules and the roles. Built before any real sale, so every sale line has its VAT record from the first day.
  10. ADR-008: Inventory sync
    Last: figures go to marketplaces only once links, the ledger and orders are proven in shadow mode.
  11. ADR-012: Billing and plans
    Beside the stock path, not in it: nothing above waits for it, and it never stops orders or stock. It must work before the first trial ends and the first seller pays.
  12. ADR-002: Data retention and archiving
    Archiving is switched on by its trigger (a cell past 500 GB, or the start of year 2), not at launch.
