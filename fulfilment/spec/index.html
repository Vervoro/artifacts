For The developer and coding agents building it, and the person reviewing their work

The rules to build ADR-014 from: the Fulfilment module. Every stock change below is a call to an Inventory port defined in ADR-006 SPEC 7, and every status change a call to Orders (ADR-007). Where this page and the ADR disagree, the ADR wins and this page is fixed. Words are defined in the map and glossary.

0. At launch and later

1. The module

  1. Always on, outside the core. Needs Orders, Inventory, Buyer data, Products (a product's facts and codes) and Access (a person's role and warehouse scope) (ADR-000 SPEC 4; owner delegation, 3 November 2026). The core never calls it.
  2. Its tables, with the fulfilment_ prefix (the module's id, ADR-001 SPEC 5), declared through tenantTable() and queried through the typed query builder: fulfilment_pick_task, fulfilment_packing, fulfilment_shipment, fulfilment_put_back, fulfilment_return with its lines as a child table, fulfilment_inspection (one row per decision on a return line), and fulfilment_command, the floor commands' keys. fulfilment_shipment holds the order, its number of boxes, method and carrier, with its lines (fulfilment_shipment_line: line, quantity, box) as a child table; the tracking number Vervoro is given goes to Buyer data's port (ADR-009 SPEC 4) (ruled by delegation, 3 November 2026; for the owner's confirmation).
  3. One transaction per floor command. Fulfilment writes its own row and calls Inventory's (and, for a shipment, Orders') port inside the same transaction, under the company's lock, with the command's key (ADR-006 SPEC 12).
  4. Screens: Orders → pick and pack, returns, and the recall list.

2. Picking

  1. Starting to pick an order creates a pick task. For each reservation it asks Inventory to claim a spot: the spot of that batch with the fewest units first, less what other open pick tasks already claim from it, unless the caller names one (Inventory's claim port, ADR-006 SPEC 7.1; owner delegation, 30 October 2026). A person may start several orders in one tap; each starts as its own command, so a held order is refused alone (owner delegation, 3 November 2026).
  2. The pick list names the batch, its lot and date, and the spot. A pick task writes no ledger entry.
  3. "Not found" calls Inventory's quarantine port (ADR-006 SPEC 7.7); the pick list then shows the new batch, or the line as short. The walk says where "not found" leads before the tap: the spot of the batch the reservation would move to, or the line short.
  4. A unit taken from a spot the books do not show is reported as it is; Inventory records it as a move or a find (ADR-006 SPEC 7.8).
  5. An order whose line is waiting or short does not join the pick list (ADR-007).

3. Packing

Packing (ADR-007's "packed") is a server command, keyed by the packing id. For each unit it names the batch:

  1. One eligible batch of the product in the spot (or the warehouse, without spots): implied.
  2. Several: for a use-by product, or a line of an order to a business buyer, the packer taps the date or lot on the unit, or scans a barcode that carries it. A product barcode alone names no batch. A barcode carries a lot when it is a GS1 element string: with a symbology identifier (]C1, ]d2, ]Q3), a leading FNC1, bracketed AIs, or more than 14 digits opening with AI 01; a plain 8 to 14 digit number is the product's GTIN and names no batch. Any other product packs as its reserved batch, marked "batch assumed", unless a scanned barcode carries the lot (owner ruling, 25 October 2026).
  3. Another eligible batch than planned (in date, not blocked, meeting the demand's need) is taken without a manager; Inventory moves or exchanges the reservation. Choices of a later date than planned are listed daily, by the company's day (ADR-000 SPEC 13.2), for a manager.
  4. Nothing to exchange, or nobody says (the packer marks it packed without tapping): the line keeps its reserved batch, marked "batch assumed", with any lot tapped.
  5. Packed units move, still reserved, to the packing spot. The orders with units there for more than 24 hours are counted on Home and on Today, opening their list.
  6. A use-by unit on or after its date, or of a blocked lot, is refused at packing and swapped.
  7. What the packing screen shows. The packing screen shows, through Buyer data's port, the order note, the gift message and customisation and, on a manual order to be delivered, the recipient's name, address and phone (ADR-009 SPEC 4 and 7.2); Amazon's gift flag says to print the message from Seller Central (ADR-004.2 SPEC 8). The pick list shows no buyer field (owner delegation, 3 November 2026).
  8. A line short at packing refuses "packed": the order goes back to waiting with the reason, as an order with a short line waits (2.5, 4.3).

4. Shipments

  1. One shipment per order. It carries every unit still to ship, possibly in several boxes, and names the lines, quantities, carrier and tracking. The packer chooses "label on the marketplace" (the default on a marketplace order) or "own carrier", which takes a carrier and, per box, a tracking number where there is one, and sends one dispatch notice per box. With several boxes the packer says which box each line goes in; a line's units may split across boxes; each box carries at least one unit, and each line's units across its boxes are every unit it still ships. A manual order ships by own carrier ("Own delivery" or "Collected" allowed), with no notice. A box without tracking goes as the marketplace allows (ADR-004.2 SPEC 9) (owner delegation, 3 November 2026).
  2. Confirming it, in one transaction, keyed by the shipment id: Inventory's dispatch port deducts exactly what was packed for it; Orders records the status shipped and, for an own-carrier shipment, the outbox note for the dispatch notice through the egress gate (ADR-000 SPEC 7). A repeat returns "already confirmed".
  3. A line never ships more than it reserved, so an order with a short line waits. A bundle line ships whole sets.
  4. Part shipped only when the marketplace reports part shipped outside Vervoro (ADR-007); the rest ships whole.
  5. Dispatch without packing in Vervoro (confirmed at the bench) is allowed from an open, picking or packed order: Inventory packs by the dispatch itself (owner delegation, 3 November 2026).
  6. Shipped outside Vervoro is Orders' import calling Inventory directly; Fulfilment shows the task asking which lot left, and a manager may reverse it ("Not from our stock").
  7. Sellable does not change on dispatch, so it sends Stock sync no note.
  8. A label bought on the marketplace (owner ruling, 22 October 2026). The shipment closes when both have happened, in either order: the order is packed in Vervoro, and the marketplace has reported its label (ADR-007 SPEC 17.8). It deducts exactly what was packed, line by line, with its batches, and sends no dispatch notice. By default the packer scans the label on the order being packed; a label of another order is refused and closes nothing; a label not yet reported says so and asks the marketplace again; a scan after the close clears "label unchecked" (ruled by delegation, 3 November 2026; for the owner's confirmation). A shipment closed without a scan is marked "label unchecked"; the list shows the label-unchecked shipments of the last 3 days. A voided label leaves the order "label bought"; the next report replaces the tracking.
  9. In shadow Fulfilment works the same; the dispatch notice is recorded, not sent, and the ship step says so (ADR-000 SPEC 7.2) (owner delegation, 3 November 2026).

5. Cancellations on the floor

  1. A confirmed cancellation is Orders' (a request releases nothing). Inventory releases the line's units.
  2. Picked, not packed: the units are in a tote and still on their spot by the books. A put-back task stays open until each unit is confirmed back, by a scan or a tap.
  3. Packed: the units are sellable in the packing spot. The put-back task moves each one to a storage spot when confirmed.
  4. After dispatch: nothing moves; a parcel that comes back is a return.

6. Returns

  1. Receiving names the order line; Inventory takes the units back into one of the batches that line dispatched, held (ADR-006 SPEC 7.9). The person chooses a storage spot of the order's warehouse (a returns bench, or by default the default spot). If the product tracks lots, the person scans or picks the lot from those the line received; another lot is refused. A bundle returns by its parts. An order with no dispatch record (shipped before Vervoro) is received line by line with a reason, and a dated product's date, into a new batch, at most what each line sold less cancelled units and earlier returns; in an order Vervoro shipped, a line it never dispatched takes none. An order not shipped is refused.
  2. Inspection decides per unit: restock (sellable again unless the batch is expired, short-dated or blocked), keep as damaged, or write off with a reason.
  3. A use-by unit is restocked only with a manager's reason; otherwise it is kept as damaged or written off.
  4. Refunds are done in the channel's own tools; the credit note is ADR-011's. Receiving a return, and a "damaged" decision, say so with the channel's deadline: on eBay, refund within 2 working days of the return arriving, or eBay refunds in full by itself and a damage deduction is lost; on Amazon, in Seller Central; a manual order says nothing (the completeness review of phase 5.8).
  5. The nightly check. Each night, at the company's night, per company with a return received in the last two days (a missed night is covered by the next), return lines equal their returned entries (through Inventory's port); a difference alerts the operator (owner delegation, 3 November 2026).

7. The recall list

  1. From a lot number, Inventory's lot trace gives the demands that received it (ADR-006 SPEC 10). Fulfilment joins each with its order: the order id, the date and the order's destination country (ADR-007 SPEC 5), never the buyer's name or address.
  2. The lines that may have received it, as the lot trace defines them (ADR-006 SPEC 10.1), are listed apart, and so are lines shipped "label unchecked" (section 4.8).
  3. The stock of that lot on hand is shown per warehouse, with a button to block the lot through Inventory's port (a manager; owner delegation, 3 November 2026).
  4. The seller reaches buyers through the marketplace's own messaging, with the order id. The list downloads as a file: an export without buyer data (ADR-010 SPEC 4), each download in the audit log; an operator takes none. Procedure: Run a recall.

8. Who may do what

Roles are ADR-010's. A person limited to some warehouses acts only on their orders.

Inspect: write off; restock a use-by unit with a reason No Yes

Database tests run on real Postgres under the runtime role (ADR-000 SPEC 15).

Packing One eligible batch is implied; several need a tap for a use-by product or a business buyer's line, and otherwise pack as the reserved batch, "batch assumed", unless a scanned barcode carries the lot; a product barcode alone never names a batch; another eligible batch moves or exchanges the reservation; nothing to exchange ends "batch assumed"

Marketplace label Packing then the label report, or the report then packing, closes one shipment that deducts what was packed and writes no dispatch notice; an unscanned close is marked "label unchecked"

Returns A return enters held, into a batch the line dispatched, at most what it dispatched; inspection outcomes write the right entries
Recall The list includes bundles and lists "batch assumed" lines apart; it shows no buyer name or address
Boundaries Fulfilment writes no inventory_ or order table; every stock change goes through an Inventory port

How to check it was built right

For someone reviewing code an AI wrote. Each line says where to look.

  • Writers: Fulfilment inserts only into fulfilment_ tables; stock and status changes are port calls (search the module for inventory_ and order tables).
  • One transaction: each floor command writes its row and calls the ports inside one transaction with its key.
  • Packing: the batch rule (implied, tap, scan) is one function; there is no path that packs without naming a batch or marking "batch assumed".
  • Shipment: one shipment per order; the confirm command refuses an order with a short or waiting line.
  • Recall: the query reads the order's country; it selects no Buyer data field.
  • No outside call in a transaction; the dispatch notice goes through the egress gate after commit.
  • Later parts: no serial field, no marketplace-warehouse handover and no Vendor shipment at launch.
