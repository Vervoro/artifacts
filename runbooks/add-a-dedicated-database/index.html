Runbook: Add a dedicated database
Built Later: The first enterprise contract that needs one (SPEC 0)
Record ADR-001, rules in SPEC 8
Who runs it The operator, when an enterprise customer needs a database of its own
Role or credential As add a cell. For an existing company, the lifecycle role in the operations job

Steps

  1. Create a Neon project of its own for the company, in the EU unless the contract names another region.
  2. Outside the EU: also set up the Worker placement, object-storage bucket, Durable Object jurisdiction, logs and backup location for that region (SPEC 8).
  3. Follow add a cell steps 2 to 9, registering kind = dedicated with a cap of 1.
  4. Place the company:
    • a new company: add it straight into this database (sign-up or the operator's enterprise form, naming the database);
    • an existing company: move it here.
  5. Once the company is in, the cell is at its cap, and placement sets it closed.

Verify

  • As in add a cell.
  • Control routes the company to the dedicated database, and the company signs in and sees its data.

If it stops halfway

  • Before step 4: as in add a cell.
  • During a move: follow move a company.
