Runbooks

The operator's procedures, one page each, with numbered steps. Each page says who runs it, which role or credential it uses, how to verify it, and what to do if it stops halfway. A runbook carries out a record; it decides nothing. Where a runbook and its record disagree, the record wins.

ADR-000: System architecture

Rules: ADR-000 SPEC.

Procedure When
Deploy and roll back New code or migrations go out, or a release must be undone

ADR-001: Multi-tenant database architecture

Rules: ADR-001 SPEC.

Procedure When
Add a cell The system recommends a new cell, or a trial cell (Later)
Add a dedicated database An enterprise customer needs a database of its own (Later)
Move a company Rebalancing, dedicated placement, or a paying trial leaving a trial cell (Later)
Export a company The seller asks for their data
Erase a company A privacy request, or 30 days after the company closes
Restore a company: look back A seller made a mistake, such as one bad bulk edit
Restore a company: full rewind A company's data is corrupt
Restore a whole cell A cell is lost
Drills Weekly, monthly and nightly, unattended
Use the owner role An emergency that no other role can fix
