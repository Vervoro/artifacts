Runbook: Set up the Stripe account
Built At launch (ruled by delegation, 5 November 2026, for the owner's confirmation)
Record ADR-012, rules in SPEC 11, SPEC 12, SPEC 15, SPEC 17 and SPEC 22
Who runs it The owner, once for each Stripe account (the sandbox staging uses, then the live account), before the first company subscribes there; again after any change to these settings
Role or credential Stripe's dashboard with the owner's own login and second factor. The app's Worker configuration for each environment, changed through a reviewed deploy

Vervoro's code relies on these account settings and never makes them itself: Stripe holds them. Every step names what to check afterwards. Run them in order on the sandbox first.

Steps

  1. Failed payments end in "unpaid". In Stripe's billing settings for failed payments, choose to mark the subscription unpaid after the last retry, never to cancel it (SPEC 15.3). Verify: the setting reads "mark the subscription as unpaid"; in the sandbox, a test subscription whose retries all fail shows the status unpaid, and its open invoice can still be paid.
  2. Retries. Smart Retries over 2 weeks for cards; Direct Debit (Bacs) retries follow Stripe's own fixed limits (2 retries over 30 days), so check they are on, under local payment methods (SPEC 15.1). Verify: the card retry schedule reads Smart Retries within 2 weeks, and retries for Bacs Direct Debit are on under local payment methods.
  3. Stripe's own emails. Turn on the customer emails for a trial ending, a failed payment, a card expiring, and a payment that needs authentication (SPEC 11.1). If a kind is not offered in the email settings, tell the operator: Vervoro's own notices then cover it (SPEC 11.2). Verify: the dashboard's own list of customer emails shows each kind it offers on; any of the four it does not offer is reported to the operator.
  4. The customer portal. Turn the portal on for changing the payment method and billing details and reading invoices. Turn cancelling off (it is on by default); leave plan switching off (it is off by default) (SPEC 12). Verify: a sandbox customer's portal shows the payment method, billing details and invoices, and offers no plan change and no cancel.
  5. Invoices in Vervoro's name. Set Vervoro's registered name, address and VAT number on invoices, with one invoice prefix and numbering across the whole account, never reset (SPEC 17.1, 17.2). Verify: a sandbox invoice shows Vervoro's name, address and VAT number, and its number carries the prefix and follows the account's last.
  6. The VAT rate. Create one tax rate: VAT, GB, 20 %, exclusive (not included in the price). A tax rate's percentage and country can't be changed after it is created: a wrong one is archived and a new one made. Set its id (txr_…) as the Worker variable STRIPE_VAT_RATE in that environment's configuration, and deploy (SPEC 17.4). Until it is set, subscribing is refused and the operator alerted; no invoice is ever made without VAT. Verify: Stripe shows the rate active, 20 %, exclusive, jurisdiction GB; after the deploy, a sandbox subscription's first invoice adds 20 % VAT on top of the plan's price.

Verify

  • Every step's own check has passed on this account.
  • In the sandbox, one test company runs end to end: Checkout, a subscription at the trial's end with VAT on its invoice, a failed payment retried then left unpaid, and the portal offering no plan change or cancel.

If it stops halfway

Each step is a setting that can be set again; none charges anyone. Until step 6's variable is deployed, subscribing is refused, so no seller is charged without VAT. Finish the steps before telling the operator the account is ready.
